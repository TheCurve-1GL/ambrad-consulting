Why it matters

Good engineers, scattered information

Most owners and operators have capable engineers and plenty of data. The trouble sits in the joins: condition data that never feeds the investment plan, a strategy that doesn't reach the maintenance programme, and decisions the board can't trace back to evidence.

ISO 55001 describes what a joined-up asset management system looks like. We help you get there in steps sized to your organisation, and we price the gaps that matter most so you know where to start.

Questions

Common questions

What is ISO 55001?

The international standard for asset management systems. It sets out what an organisation needs in place to manage its assets for value: policy, objectives, plans, the right information, competent people and a way of checking it all works.

What is a Strategic Asset Management Plan (SAMP)?

The document that links your organisation's objectives to your asset management objectives and plans. It explains how decisions about assets are made, so the board, the regulator and the people doing the work can see the line of sight.

Can you certify us to ISO 55001?

No, and that's deliberate. We're not a certification body, so we can be candid about where you stand and help you close the gaps before an assessor or certification audit.

Which sectors do you work in?

Highways and transport, rail, aviation, and water and energy networks. The common thread is owners and operators running large portfolios of long-life assets.
