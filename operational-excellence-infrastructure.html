Operational excellence for infrastructure operations and maintenance

Operations and maintenance is where an asset earns its keep, and where most of its whole-life cost is spent. We help O&M teams and their clients run the work safely, hit the contract measures and spend less fixing the same things twice.

Why it matters

The same faults, every winter

On many O&M contracts the inspection and maintenance regime was set at the start and never revisited. Crews follow it faithfully, but the faults that cost the most keep coming back, and performance reports take days to pull together.

We look at the regime, the data and the contract measures together. The aim is simple: the right work, done once, with records that stand up when the client, the regulator or an auditor asks.

Questions

Common questions

What does operational excellence mean in O&M?

Running and maintaining the network safely and reliably, at a fair cost, and getting a bit better every year. In practice that's the right regime, clear records, named owners and a habit of fixing root causes.

Who do you work with?

Asset owners, O&M contractors and PFI or PPP project companies, in highways, rail, airports, water and energy.

Can this help reduce deductions?

Often, yes. Many deductions come from repeat faults, late reporting or missing records. Fixing those tends to protect income as well as performance.
